--About PeerLynx--
PeerLynx is an institution-based peer-to-peer skill development platform designed to help students connect, learn, and share knowledge within the same campus-based environment. The platform creates opportunities for students to discover peers with valuable skills, strengths, and learning experience that can support their personal and educational growth.

PeerLynx provides a structured environment where users can create profiles, showcase their skills and interests, and interact with a community of learners. The platform also includes intelligent recommendation features that suggest suitable peers based on shared interests, learning goals, and user activities.

The system promotes collaboration, knowledge sharing, and student engagement while making peer learning more accessible, organized, and effective within tertiary institutions
